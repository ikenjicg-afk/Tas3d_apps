How it goes

Show the game — the screen shows the picture in heavy render noise; every checked-in phone gets a big RENDER button. Start rendering — 3, 2, 1 on the screen and the phones, then everybody taps: every tap is a sample, the noise goes away. It takes as long as the hall needs (fewer people tapping — longer); at 100% the clean picture appears — “TAS3D 2026 is open” — with the teams (Visualisers, Architects …) and who rendered most. Finish now if the hall gets tired. Close the game puts the logo back.
